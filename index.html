Original explanations,
ready for their audience.

Roots Video Publisher helps the channel owner prepare original commentary videos and upload them to the connected Roots channel.

Prepare

Research, narration, animation and editing bring each explanation together.

Check

Channel identity, source credits and video metadata are checked before upload.

Publish

Uploads follow the owner's chosen visibility and release settings, subject to YouTube's permissions.

One owner, one connected channel

This is a privately operated creator tool for Roots (@Roots0to1). It is not offered as a public sign-in service. Visitors do not need to connect a Google account.

Google account access

The owner grants permission to view the connected YouTube account and manage video uploads. The tool uses channel information to confirm the destination and upload status to check the result. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive files or viewer watch history.

Read the privacy policy for how account access and application records are handled.
