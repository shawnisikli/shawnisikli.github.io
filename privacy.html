Privacy policy

Prepared October 3, 2026

Who operates this application

Roots Video Publisher is a privately operated creator tool for the owner of the Roots YouTube channel, @Roots0to1. It is not a public account-connection service. Contact the operator at shawnisikli@gmail.com with questions about this application or its records.

Information used

After the owner authorizes Google account access, the tool uses the connected YouTube channel's ID, name and handle to verify the upload destination. It sends the owner's video files, titles, descriptions, source credits, thumbnails when supplied, visibility settings and release settings to YouTube. It may read the uploaded video's owner, status and processing information to verify the result.

The application requests YouTube upload and read-only permissions. It does not request Gmail or Google Drive access, and it does not collect viewer watch histories, advertising profiles or private viewer messages.

Account credentials

Google access and refresh credentials are stored in files on the owner's computer, outside the application's source repository. The uploader uses them to authenticate requests to Google. Credentials are not included in the public website or in the uploader's application logs. The owner controls the computer and its access.

Uses and service providers

Google account information is used to operate and verify the owner's video uploads. Uploaded content and associated metadata are sent to Google/YouTube and are subject to the selected visibility setting and Google's Privacy Policy.

The owner's automation assistant may process channel identifiers, upload status and job metadata as part of operating the workflow. Scripts and creative material may also be processed by the owner's selected AI services for research, narration and illustration. This application does not provide Google user data to advertisers or data brokers, and does not sell Google account information.

Storage and retention

The application stores authorization credentials, uploaded-video IDs, upload receipts and verification records on the owner's computer. Upload receipts help prevent accidental duplicate uploads. The current application does not automatically erase those records after a fixed interval. The owner can remove the local records and revoke authorization when the integration is no longer needed. Removing local records does not delete videos already stored on YouTube.

Revoking access and requesting removal

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections, then remove the application's local credential files. For help locating or removing application records, contact the operator using the address above. Manage or delete uploaded videos through the channel's YouTube controls.

This website

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. These pages do not add advertising, analytics scripts or application cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and processes website requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The channel's Google authorization credentials and upload records are not stored on this website.

Changes

The operator will update this page when the application's data practices change. Any material change in the account permissions requested will require appropriate authorization through Google.
